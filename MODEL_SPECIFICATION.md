# Model specification

The event process uses four context types: ordinary truck, truck--vulnerable-road-user, truck--bus and tanker--community. Correlated latent states and region groups affect context and the 20 latent factors. Vehicle counts, bus occupants and community exposure are sampled with role constraints. A community exposure is retained only when fire, hazardous release or crowd collision creates a physical pathway.

Fatalities, serious injuries, minor injuries and unhurt people are generated by exposed role using beta-binomial draws. Property damage, casualty-related expenditure and response/aftermath cost are generated as separate positive components with event-level shocks. The reported loss is their sum with mixed reporting precision. The label uses the fixed threshold rule documented in `data/CODEBOOK.md`.

The primary assessment mask depends on event complexity, context and report quality. It does not directly read fatalities or economic loss, but can remain associated with severity through shared event complexity. The severity-linked extension adds a separate label-dependent recording stress test. `recorded_features.csv` equals `model_factors.csv * assessment_mask.csv`. Complete latent factors, contexts and masks are diagnostics, not primary predictors.

The complete numerical mechanism and factor coefficients for this simulation study are given in Section 2 of the article's supplementary material. PROTOCOL_NOTES.md resolves terminology in the frozen JSON: legacy context_probabilities are unused; v3.2 uses softmax context_logits. The recording variables describe post-event information; exclusion of direct casualty and loss inputs does not imply statistical independence from severity.
